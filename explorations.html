01 — Films / Director

Soil Story

Director202516mmExperimental Documentary

Soil Story is a 16mm film, combining found foliage and an experimental approach to the film developing process that is in collaboration with the environment. The film explores the juxtaposing histories and memories of the Ridgewood Reservoir and the Cemetery Belt. Two locations that straddle each other with the Jackie Robinson Parkway cut in between.

With both locations in conversation with each other, we explore the cycles of time, space, memory and land development and disposal.

Screenings

Seeing Things, Dreaming Things

Director / Co-creator202116mm + Hi-8 TapePersonal Documentary

Seeing Things, Dreaming Things is a personal documentary that explores memories and dreams, in an effort to organize these subjective and personal experiences into something more logical and concrete.

03 — Films / Cinematographer · Producer · Colorist

Altar Piece

Cinematographer / Producer / Colorist2025Documentary

The Holy Mary amidst ongoing traffic. A basement of deities with the light on 24/7. A collection of trash, or so it seems.

“Altar Piece” explores Ridgewood, Queens, through the altars and shrines that ornament the neighborhood. While some are more recognizable in form than others, these objects are brought together with a considered touch.

Directed by Kiana Fernandez. Cinematography by Olivia Acuña.

05 — Films / Cinematographer

In Love, In Time

Cinematographer201916mmShort Film

In Love, In Time, follows a narrative of a woman who is in two different relationships during two periods of her life, but the emotions that the relationships produce are the same. The film is meant to explore the idea of relationship dependency amongst youth, and one’s identity solely being tied to another.

Directed by Philip Thompson. Cinematography by Olivia Acuna. 16mm.

09 — Programming

Alcove Cinema

Co-founder · Programmer2026 – presentMicrocinemaBrooklyn Peace Center, Bed-Stuy

Alcove Cinema is a microcinema at the Brooklyn Peace Center, 23 Marcus Garvey Blvd in Bedford-Stuyvesant, that Olivia co-founded in January 2026 and programs. It hosts one to two free screenings each month, showcasing the work of local New York City filmmakers, with an open call for submissions and a Q&A with filmmakers whenever they can be in the room.

Since opening night in February 2026 the series has paired films with community partners, from a seed library and seasonal newsletter to free tea on the night, and has grown to include themed programs and a works-in-progress workshop for filmmakers.

About / Contact

Olivia Acuña

Olivia Acuña is a filmmaker, archivist, projectionist, and programmer based in New York City. Her work is personal, exploratory, and handmade, often taking the form of the personal essay and experimental documentary. She explores memory, dreams, history, and intimacy, working primarily with analog film and experimenting with alternative processing techniques.

Olivia's films have screened in New York, Chicago, and Los Angeles. In 2024, she participated in an eight-month documentary residency at UnionDocs, where she produced two short documentaries, Soil Story and Altar Piece.

In January 2026, Olivia co-founded Alcove Cinema, a microcinema at the Brooklyn Peace Center in Bedford-Stuyvesant. Alcove hosts one to two free screenings each month, showcasing the work of local New York City filmmakers and fostering a space for independent cinema and community.
